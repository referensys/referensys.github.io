Installing on Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome on your phone or tablet and tap Download for Android.
  2. Tap Open when the download finishes. If Android says Chrome isn't allowed to install apps, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Referensys, sign in with Google, and turn on tap recording when it asks.
Installing the Chrome extension
  1. Click Download for Chrome. Extract the zip somewhere you'll keep it, like Documents. Chrome runs the extension from that folder, so don't delete it.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode at the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the Referensys folder inside what you extracted (the one with manifest.json in it).
  4. Referensys opens in a tab. Pin its icon using the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar, then sign in with Google.
  5. When there's a new version, Referensys tells you and walks you through updating.
Installing on Windows
  1. Click Download for Windows and open the file.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Referensys opens when it's installed, and updates itself from then on.
Installing on a phone or tablet?
Point its camera at this code to open this page there.

How it works

  1. Click Record and do the task as you normally would. Each click or tap becomes a step.
  2. Cover personal details with redaction boxes and check each step's wording.
  3. Save to Google Drive. Each SOP is one PDF, and changes save automatically after that.
Privacy policySaves only to your own Google Drive. Referensys has no servers of its own.
