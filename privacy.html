Referensys privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What Referensys does with your data

Referensys records screenshots and step descriptions on your own computer so you can build SOPs. Screenshots stay on your computer until you choose to save.

Google Drive

When you sign in with Google, Referensys asks for two permissions: to see, create, edit and delete only the files Referensys itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope), and to keep its own settings in a hidden app folder in your Drive that only Referensys can see (the drive.appdata scope). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Referensys uses these permissions only to save, open, rename and list SOPs in a "Referensys SOPs" folder in your Drive, and to keep the list it uses for search in its hidden app folder. Before uploading, Referensys permanently applies your redaction boxes to each screenshot, and it won't upload a step until you have confirmed its privacy check.

What we don't do

  • Referensys has no servers of its own. Your SOPs go directly from your computer to your Google Drive.
  • We don't collect, sell, share or analyze your data, and we don't use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Referensys's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Updates

The apps check GitHub for new versions of themselves. That check sends no personal data.

Removing access

You can remove Referensys's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your SOPs stay in your Drive until you delete them.
